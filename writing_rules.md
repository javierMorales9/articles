# Writing Rules

These rules complement `workflow.md`. They are split by purpose so article work can use the right kind of guidance at the right time.

Use:

- `content_rules.md` for article substance: structure, examples, context, stakes, and explanation design.
- `style_rules.md` for prose voice: tone, pacing, sentence-level style, and AI-pattern avoidance.

## How To Apply Them

When creating or revising `series.md`, `draft.md`, or `article.md`, follow both rule files. Use `content_rules.md` to keep the explanation grounded, and use `style_rules.md` to keep the prose direct, concrete, first-person, and human from the beginning.

`draft.md` is allowed to be rough, but it should not use AI filler as scaffolding. A rough paragraph with the right voice is easier to turn into an article than a polished-sounding paragraph that says nothing.

Do not let `style_rules.md` override technical accuracy. If a section needs careful qualification, keep the qualification, but write it plainly.
